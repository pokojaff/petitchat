Signa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Signa is designed as a personal self-discovery tool. Everything you record is stored privately on your device, and we do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or any personal data on our servers.

Information We Store
  • Astro Journal entries: Your daily reflections across Physical, Emotional, Mental, Relationships, and Work dimensions.
  • Biorhythm values: Generated locally from your birth information and daily cosmic data.
  • Optional menstrual cycle data: Period start dates and calculated average cycle length, if you choose to log them.
How Your Data Is Used

All data powers in-app features such as wave visualisations, correlation insights, and historical timelines. The information never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it (for example, through system share sheets).

Data Sharing

We do not transmit, sell, or share your personal information with third parties. Signa operates without third-party analytics SDKs or advertising networks.

Data Retention & Control

Journal entries, biorhythm data, and cycle logs remain on your device until you delete the app or remove the entries yourself. You can clear journal history or cycle information at any time from within the app.

Children’s Privacy

Signa is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
