The Challenge

Three data streams.

Managing blood glucose or optimizing athletic performance requires understanding three things simultaneously: what you ate, how you moved, and how your body responded.

Today, that information is scattered across a meal planning app, a CGM companion app, and a fitness tracker. MenuBuilder brings it together into a single weekly view — correlating meal nutrition, real-time CGM glucose curves, and Garmin activity data on a shared timeline.

Garmin Integration

Why Garmin data matters for metabolic health

Glucose response is not driven by food alone. Physical activity, cardiovascular stress, and recovery state all significantly influence blood sugar levels — yet this context is absent from most CGM dashboards.

A 45-minute cycling session at 2 p.m. can meaningfully lower glucose two hours later. Without knowing the activity happened, that drop looks unexplained. With Garmin data, the pattern becomes actionable.

Requested data points & purpose
Data point Why MenuBuilder uses it
Activity summaries type, start time, duration, calories burned Displayed as event markers on the glucose + nutrition timeline, enabling direct visual correlation between exercise sessions and glycemic response. Core feature of the Garmin integration.
Resting heart rate Tracks cardiovascular fitness trends over time alongside dietary changes and activity load — a reliable indicator of improving metabolic health.
HRV / Body Battery Physiological stress (derived from HRV) raises cortisol, which in turn raises blood glucose. HRV provides an objective explanatory layer for glucose variability not accounted for by food or exercise alone.
Body weight Correlates meal plans and activity load with weight trends over time, supporting weight-management goals alongside glycemic control.
VO₂ max Fitness level indicator contextualizing activity intensity relative to the user's aerobic capacity — relevant for personalized exercise guidance and progress tracking.
Sleep duration & quality Sleep deprivation measurably impairs insulin sensitivity, causing elevated fasting glucose. Sleep data adds a meaningful explanatory layer when morning glucose is unexpectedly high without a dietary cause.

Privacy & Security

Your data is yours. Always.

MenuBuilder stores meal plans, glucose readings, and activity data exclusively in association with the authenticated user's account. Data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles outside the platform.

Encrypted credentials

LibreLinkUp passwords and OAuth tokens are stored encrypted using Fernet AES-128. Plaintext credentials never touch the database.

User-controlled connections

Connections to LibreLinkUp and Garmin are configured individually by each user and can be revoked at any time from profile settings, with immediate data deletion.

Isolated data storage

All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database accessible only to the authenticated account owner. Health data is never accessible across accounts.

MenuBuilder Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

MenuBuilder is designed as a personal health and nutrition tool. All user data is stored on secure servers and is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than providing the service to the authenticated user.

Information We Store
  • Account data: Email address and hashed password.
  • Meal plans and recipes: Your planned meals, recipe collections, and shopping lists.
  • Glucose readings: CGM data fetched from FreeStyle Libre via LibreLinkUp (retained for up to 365 days).
  • Activity data: Garmin activity summaries, heart rate, body weight, HRV, VO₂ max, and sleep data — fetched via the Garmin Health API with explicit user consent.
  • Third-party credentials: LibreLinkUp passwords and Garmin OAuth tokens, stored encrypted using Fernet AES-128. Plaintext credentials are never stored.
How Your Data Is Used

Data is used exclusively to provide in-app features: generating weekly meal and nutrition overviews, displaying CGM glucose curves, and visualizing Garmin activity data on the shared health timeline. No data is processed for advertising or profiling purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not transmit, sell, or share personal information with third parties. MenuBuilder operates without third-party analytics SDKs or advertising networks.

Data Retention & Control

Users may delete their account at any time, which permanently removes all associated data. Glucose readings and food logs are automatically purged after 365 days. Users may disconnect third-party services (LibreLinkUp, Garmin) individually at any time; disconnection triggers immediate deletion of the corresponding cached data.

Children's Privacy

MenuBuilder is intended for users 16 years of age or older and is not directed at children.
