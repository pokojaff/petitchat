Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Petit chat, s.r.o. builds and operates software products. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data in the products we run ourselves, including products that process fitness or health-related data. A product may publish a shorter notice with details that apply only to that product. Software we build for a client and do not operate is covered by the client's own privacy policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the product, we process:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Content you add: Records you create in the product, such as plans, journal entries, settings, and similar material the product is built to store.
  • Health, biometric, and activity data: Where a product includes it, measurements and activity you enter or that we receive from a sensor, device, or cloud service you connect. Examples include heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, blood oxygen, stress, glucose, activity type, duration, intensity, and location tracks.
  • Context you choose to share: Optional information you give so a feature can be calibrated, including health conditions where the product asks for them.

We request only what that product needs. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

2. Sensors, Devices & Cloud Services

Some products connect to sensors, wearable devices, and cloud services you choose to link, and harvest the data those sources make available. Examples include Garmin, Apple, Abbott, and Dexcom. When you authorize a connection, we receive information through that provider's secure APIs or other authorized channels, and only in the scope the product needs. You can revoke access at any time in the provider's account or device settings, or in the product where it offers a disconnect control.

3. How We Protect Data
  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Isolation: One user's data is not available to another user, except where the product has an explicit relationship you take part in, such as a coach and an athlete.
  • No sale: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or health data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Location: Personal data is stored and processed in the European Union / EEA, in line with the GDPR. A connected provider may process data on its own systems outside the EU; that is governed by the provider's terms.

Operational detail — backups, access by staff, retention, and deletion — is in our Data Management & Security Policy.

4. How Long We Keep Data & Your Rights

We keep personal data while your account is active, or for a shorter period the product states for a given category. Under the GDPR you can:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate.
  • Delete your account and the personal data that belongs to it.
  • Export your data in a structured, machine-readable format where the product supports it.
  • Withdraw consent for a connected source or for optional processing, without affecting processing that already had another legal basis.
  • Complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ) or to your local supervisory authority.
5. Children's Privacy

Our products are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without verifiable parental consent.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: support@petitchat.cz.
