Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

At Petit chat, s.r.o., we build advanced monitoring systems for athletes with specific health considerations. Your trust is our most valuable asset. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal, fitness, and health-related data. Our systems are designed with a "Security by Design" philosophy to ensure the highest level of protection for sensitive medical and performance metrics.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we process the following categories of data:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Health & Biometric Data: Heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep patterns, blood oxygen levels (SpO2), and stress levels.
  • Activity & Fitness Data: Type of activity, duration, intensity, GPS tracks, and recovery metrics synced from third-party platforms (e.g., Garmin Connect).
  • Medical Context: Optional information you provide regarding your specific health conditions to better calibrate our analytical models.
2. Third-Party Integrations (Garmin Connect)

Our application integrates with the Garmin Connect Developer Program. When you authorize our application to access your Garmin data, we receive information via secure APIs. We only request the minimum scope of permissions necessary for our monitoring features. You can revoke this access at any time through your Garmin Connect account settings or within our application.

3. Data Security & Management

We implement industry-leading technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data:

  • Strong Encryption: All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3 protocols.
  • Data Isolation: Each user's data is stored in logically separated environments to prevent unauthorized cross-access.
  • Zero Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • Server Location: Our primary data processing occurs on secured servers located within the European Union, in full compliance with GDPR.
4. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain your data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.
  • Right to Portability: You can export your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
5. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without verifiable parental consent.
