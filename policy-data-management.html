Data Management & Security Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This document outlines the technical frameworks and operational procedures used by Petit chat, s.r.o. to ensure the integrity, availability, and confidentiality of user data.

1. Infrastructure & Data Residency

Our production environment is hosted on DigitalOcean infrastructure. To comply with GDPR and ensure low-latency access for European users, all health and personal data is stored within the Frankfurt (FRA1) data center region (Germany, EEA).

  • Server Security: We utilize firewalled droplets and VPC (Virtual Private Cloud) to isolate database traffic from the public internet.
  • Database Management: We employ managed database clusters with automated patching and encrypted storage volumes.
2. Backup & Disaster Recovery

We recognize the critical nature of health data. Our backup strategy is designed for resilience:

  • Frequency: Full system snapshots and database backups are performed once every 24 hours.
  • Redundancy: Backups are stored on separate, geographically redundant storage volumes within the DigitalOcean infrastructure.
  • Integrity: We regularly perform recovery drills to ensure that data can be restored effectively in the event of a platform-wide or application-level failure.
3. Data Retention & Deletion Logic

We give users full control over their digital footprint. Our deletion process follows a two-tier approach:

  • Standard Deletion: Upon account closure, data is placed in a "soft-delete" state for 30 days, allowing users to recover their history if the request was accidental.
  • Immediate Liquidation: Users may opt for "Immediate Permanent Deletion." In this case, all health and biometric records are purged from active production databases within minutes, without the possibility of recovery.
  • Backup Purging: Data in backups is cleared naturally as part of the standard 30-day rotation cycle.
4. Access Control & Human Factors

We minimize human interaction with sensitive data through strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

  • Database Administrators: Direct access to the database is restricted to authorized personnel whose employment contracts include specific non-disclosure and sensitive data handling clauses.
  • Assisted Support Access: Support staff cannot access user accounts by default. Access is only granted through a mutual verification process requiring the user's active assistance and explicit consent for a limited time window.
  • Authentication: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all administrative interfaces.
5. Research & Anonymization

To improve health outcomes and system performance, we may use data for statistical research. Any data used for these purposes is subjected to a rigorous anonymization process. All Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is stripped, ensuring that records can never be linked back to an individual user, thus maintaining 100% privacy even during large-scale trend analysis.

6. Security Audits

We perform regular internal security reviews of our Data Management Policy to adapt to new threats and ensure that our technical debt is managed in favor of user safety.
