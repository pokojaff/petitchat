Data Management & Security Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy describes how Petit chat, s.r.o. protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal data in the products and services we operate. It applies across our products. Where a product needs a more specific rule — for example how long a particular kind of record is kept — that rule is stated in the product's own notice. Work we deliver for a client's own systems is governed by that client's policies.

1. Where Data Is Processed

Personal data, including health, fitness, and other sensitive data where a product processes it, is stored and processed on infrastructure located in the European Union / EEA. Products may use different EU hosting providers. We select them for security, reliability, and data-protection terms, and we keep production data inside the EU.

If you connect a sensor, device, or cloud service, that provider may process data on its own systems, which can be outside the EU. That transfer is covered by the provider's terms and by the safeguards that apply to that provider. You can disconnect the source at any time in the provider's settings or, where the product offers it, in the product itself.

2. Security Measures

We apply the same baseline to every product we operate:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, using current industry-standard protocols and algorithms.
  • Least exposure: Databases and internal services are not left open to the public internet. Only the interfaces a product needs are reachable.
  • Isolation: One user's records are not available to another user. Access between accounts requires an explicit relationship the product supports, such as a coach and an athlete.
  • No sale of data: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal or health data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Minimum data: A product requests only the categories it needs to provide its features, including from connected sensors, devices, and cloud services.
3. Backups & Recovery

Production data is backed up regularly so a product can be restored after a failure. Backups stay within the EU and are kept only for as long as they are needed for recovery. We test that restoration works. A backup is a recovery copy, not a second long-term archive of your account.

4. Retention & Deletion

We keep personal data for as long as your account is active, or for the shorter period a product states for a given category. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data that belongs to it. Deletion removes data from active systems. Copies that already sit in a backup disappear when that backup expires.

Some products delete a connected source immediately when you disconnect it, or purge older readings on a fixed schedule. Those periods are part of the product notice, not of this company-wide policy.

5. Who Can Access Data

Access to production personal data is limited to people who need it to run the service, and those people are bound by confidentiality. Administrative access requires strong authentication. Support staff do not have standing access to browse health or other sensitive records. Where a product allows assisted support, that access is limited to the request you make and to the time needed to resolve it.

6. Statistics

A product may use data to improve the service or to produce statistics. When it does, the data used for that purpose is aggregated or anonymised so that it cannot reasonably be linked back to you. The product notice says whether this happens and what is retained. Anonymised statistics are not a substitute for deleting your personal data: personal data is still deleted as described above.

7. Reviews

We review these controls as products, providers, and threats change. Questions about this policy or about your data can be sent to support@petitchat.cz.
