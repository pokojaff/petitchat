Cookie and browser storage policy

MUST3 service · document version 2026-05-30 · effective 30 May 2026

This policy explains how the MUST3 web application (must3.com), operated by Petit chat s.r.o., uses cookies and similar technologies (localStorage, sessionStorage).

1. Consent banner

On your first visit we show a banner with:

  • Accept all — enables essential cookies and optional functional storage (below).
  • Essential only — enables only cookies required for sign-in; functional localStorage is not used.

Your choice is stored in localStorage under must3_cookie_consent. Change it anytime at https://must3.com/?cookie_settings=1.

2. Essential cookies

Required for the service to work (sign-in, session security). Disabling them will log you out.

Used only after you choose Accept all in the banner:

4. Technical sessionStorage

After a new app deployment, sessionStorage may be used once to reload the page if a module fails to load. It contains no personal data and lasts only for the browser session.

5. Analytics cookies

We do not use analytics cookies. Visit statistics are collected with Umami in cookieless mode. Umami does not store an identifier in your browser and is not tied to the cookie banner choice.

6. Marketing cookies

We do not use third-party marketing or advertising cookies.

7. Account consents (not cookies)

Consents for personal-data processing (privacy & terms, health-data use, newsletter) are stored in our secure database after sign-in — not in cookies. See the Privacy policy.

8. Browser controls

You can delete cookies and localStorage in browser settings. Deleting essential cookies signs you out. Deleting must3_cookie_consent shows the banner again.

9. Contact

10. Changes

We will publish updates on this page with a new version date. Material changes may require renewed consent in the app.
