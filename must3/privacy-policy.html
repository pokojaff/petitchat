Privacy Policy

MUST3 service · document version 2026-05-30 · effective 30 May 2026

This document explains how Petit chat s.r.o. (“we”, “controller”) processes personal data of users of the MUST3 web application at https://must3.com (the “service”).

MUST3 is a meal-planning and health-data platform (nutrition, wearable/sensor data, coach ↔ user collaboration). Processing complies with the GDPR and applicable EU/Czech law.

2. Data we process

  • Identity & contact — email, name, nickname, avatar (optional).
  • Account — role (user, coach, analyst), subscription, locale and unit preferences.
  • Nutrition profile — targets, smart-profile inputs (sex, birth year, height, weight, activity level), diet preferences and allergens.
  • Health & sensor data — data imported from connected devices/services (e.g. glucose, heart rate, steps, energy), cognitive mini-game results, aggregated dashboard metrics. These are special-category data under GDPR; we process them only with your explicit consent or as necessary to provide the service you requested.
  • User-generated content — meal plans, recipes, shopping lists, notes, blog likes.
  • Consent records — grants/revocations (privacy & terms, health-data use for research/statistics, newsletter) including document version and timestamp.
  • Technical data — authentication logs, IP addresses as required for security by infrastructure providers.

5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Production MUST3 data is stored in the EU. Some providers (e.g. Google, Apple) may process data outside the EEA only when you activate them (OAuth), with appropriate safeguards (SCCs, adequacy decisions, etc.).

6. Retention

  • Account data — for the life of the account, then as required by law or until deletion/anonymisation on request.
  • Health/sensor data — for the life of the account unless deleted earlier; after account deletion, anonymisation for statistics only with consent and per in-app settings.
  • Consent audit logs — as long as needed to demonstrate compliance (typically up to 10 years after withdrawal).
  • Security logs — usually up to 12 months unless law requires longer.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and to withdraw consent at any time (withdrawal does not affect lawfulness of processing before withdrawal).

Contact privacy@must3.com. We respond within 30 days.

You may lodge a complaint with the Czech Data Protection Authority (uoou.cz) or your local EU supervisory authority.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, database row-level security (RLS), role separation (user, coach, admin), and access minimisation. Passwords are managed by Supabase Auth, not stored in our application database.

9. Children

The service is not intended for persons under 16. If we learn we collected a child’s data without parental consent, we will delete it.
