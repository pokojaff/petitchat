Training, meals, and recovery.

Petit chat builds this product and will run it. An athlete and the trainer they invite share a plan. Activity, sleep, heart rate, and recovery come from the athlete’s own account, including a Garmin watch once they authorize it.

How it works
For the athlete

One place for the training plan, meals, and recovery from the devices they connect.

For the trainer

The coach they invite can see the plan and the metrics needed to adjust it. Nobody else can.

We operate it

The service runs on our systems in the EU. We are the operator.

What it does

A coaching product for an athlete and their personal trainer.

Training plan

The week of sessions, shared by the athlete and the coach.

Meals next to training

Nutrition in the same week as the workouts.

Activity and recovery

Sleep, heart rate, HRV, stress, and activity from a watch or health service the athlete connects.

Access they control

The athlete authorizes the connection and can revoke it. We do not sell the data.
