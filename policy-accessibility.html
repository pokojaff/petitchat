Accessibility Statement

Last updated: March 2026

At Petit chat, s.r.o., we are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to our software solutions.

1. Our Standards

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web and mobile content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

2. Key Features for Accessibility

In our design and development process, we prioritize the following:

  • Screen Reader Compatibility: We use semantic HTML and ARIA labels to ensure that our interfaces are navigable by screen readers.
  • Keyboard Navigation: All critical functions are accessible via keyboard shortcuts and logical tab orders.
  • Color Contrast & Typography: We maintain high contrast ratios and use scalable fonts to support users with low vision.
  • Cognitive Ease: We aim for clear, concise language and intuitive layouts to reduce cognitive load for all users.
3. Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our systems are designed to be compatible with modern assistive technologies, including common screen readers (like VoiceOver and TalkBack) and browser magnification tools.

4. Ongoing Efforts & Feedback

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment. We regularly test our applications using both automated tools and manual reviews to identify and fix potential barriers.

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our products. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please let us know:

Email: accessibility@petitchat.cz
Response time: We try to respond to feedback within 5 business days.

5. Technical Specifications

Accessibility of our platform relies on the following technologies to work with the particular combination of web browser and any assistive technologies or plugins installed on your computer: HTML, WAI-ARIA, CSS, and JavaScript.
