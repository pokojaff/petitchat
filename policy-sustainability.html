Sustainability & Digital Responsibility

Last updated: March 2026

At Petit chat, s.r.o., we believe that digital innovation should not come at the expense of our planet. As a remote-first software company, we are committed to minimizing our carbon footprint through efficient engineering and responsible infrastructure choices.

1. Green Cloud Infrastructure

The environmental impact of software is primarily driven by the energy consumption of data centers. We carefully select our hosting partners based on their commitment to renewable energy:

  • DigitalOcean Frankfurt: Our primary data center region is selected for its high standards of energy efficiency and the commitment of the local grid to transition towards carbon neutrality.
  • Resource Optimization: We use "right-sizing" for our server instances, ensuring we do not waste energy on idle computing power.
2. Sustainable Engineering (Green Code)

Efficient code is sustainable code. We strive to reduce the "digital weight" of our applications to save energy on both the server-side and the user's device:

  • Algorithmic Efficiency: We optimize our back-end processes to minimize CPU cycles and memory usage, directly reducing the electricity required for data processing.
  • Data Minimization: By requesting and storing only the necessary data points from providers like Garmin, we reduce the energy cost of data transmission and storage.
  • Battery Longevity: Our mobile integrations are designed to be "battery-aware," reducing frequent wake-ups of the user's device, which extends hardware lifespan and reduces charging frequency.
3. Remote-First & Paperless Operations

Our operational model is designed to be low-impact by default:

  • Zero Commute: As a remote-first team, we eliminate daily commuting emissions, significantly reducing our corporate carbon footprint.
  • Fully Digital Workflow: From contract signing to internal documentation, we operate 100% paperless. We prioritize digital-only tools to avoid the environmental costs of physical office supplies.
4. Hardware Lifecycle

We encourage a "repair and reuse" mindset within our team. We invest in high-quality hardware designed for longevity and ensure that any end-of-life equipment is disposed of through certified e-waste recycling programs.

5. Continuous Improvement

Sustainability is a journey. We regularly review our tech stack and operational processes to identify new ways to reduce our environmental impact as green technologies evolve.
